The hidden grid inside a stretched block

What you're looking at: a clear plastic plate with a hole, being pulled by brass grips. Seen through polarising filters, stress shows up as rainbow bands: more bands packed together means more stress.

Why it matters: the force inside a material depends on which way you slice it. A tensor is the small grid of numbers that captures all of that at once. Engineers use exactly this to find where parts will crack, and the same idea describes spacetime and the data in AI models.

How to use it

  1. Drag Pull harder and watch the rainbow crowd around the hole.
  2. Drag the glass probe anywhere on the plate.
  3. Turn the cut: the amber arrow is the pull on that slice, the cyan arrow is the slide along it.
  4. Press Turn my axes: every number on the card changes, but nothing physical does.
  5. Reset to example puts everything back.
